# Inside the Mark

Approved direction: visitors enter Fathallah Haj's real work through the enclosed
counter of his monogram. The audience remains regional businesses and startup
teams. Primary action: Start a project. Secondary action: See selected work.

## Identity and composition

Use the supplied counterform master without changing its compound path, circle,
aspect ratio, padding, or orientation. Acid #C8FF34 on ink #0B0B0B opens the page;
white #F7F7F4 and muted #B7B7B0 support it. The existing Latin, Arabic and Hebrew
fonts remain. No generated assets, new imagery, video, or WebGL are required.

The opening has a stable semantic copy plane, the actual vector mark in front,
and the Go To Nature screenshot behind the counter. The mark and image move at
independent rates. Scroll magnifies the whole mark about its counter, aligns the
opening to the viewport, and resolves to the first project image. Desktop uses
1.08 additional viewports, phones 0.65. The page uses native scrolling.

## Journey and feeling curve

1. Recognition: the complete monogram and Fathallah Haj dominate the opening.
2. Discovery: the opening in the mark grows into a real product. This is the
   only cinematic peak. The opening is quiet before motion begins.
3. Confidence: five featured projects share an interactive deck of signed prints.
   Project names stay visible while a chosen print comes forward with its real
   screenshot or architecture, summary, status and evidence. The index includes
   the remaining projects and the FocusQuest beta download.
4. Clarity: Product, AI and Infrastructure consolidate the offer and audience.
5. Connection: the working process leads to a personal inquiry, signed by the
   complete mark on white. The final screen remains stable and usable.

Tell-someone sentence: "It’s the site where you go through his logo and come out
inside something he built."

## Structure and constraints

This is a brand portal followed by a portfolio in natural flow, rather than a
continuous world or a repeated series of pinned scenes. The mark supplies the
single transition. No fake dashboard, new statistics, section counters, pointer
capture, horizontal rail, or waiting for an animation to finish. Do not add
animation to ordinary project copy or input controls to meet a device quota.

Relative to the previous homepage, the hero, content order, closing composition
and signature interaction change. Navigation destinations remain compatible.
No local Scroll Craft fingerprint registry was present during planning.

## Integration and verification

Follow Scroll Craft's design and visual verification guidance within the existing
Next.js architecture. Use a small React controller with mount/unmount cleanup;
do not modify or embed the upstream global engine, which has no destroy API.
The normal server HTML is the complete motion-off page. Enable the cinematic
layout only after the project image is loaded, and reset it on load failure or
reduced-motion changes. Route changes must not retain listeners or animation
frames. The work anchor goes directly to the project deck after the sticky stage.

The September 14 refinement replaces repeated large project sections with a
collection visitors choose from. Seeker Radar opens the deck; Go To Nature can
also be revisited there after its hero reveal. The original palette and fonts
remain. White print surfaces carry the exact black monogram, while an acid dot
identifies the selected project. Desktop pairs project names with a compact
stack; phones place a two-column selector above a print that fits its content.
Native radio inputs keep selection, arrow keys and case-study links usable
without JavaScript. Only the chosen print responds with a short arrival motion;
reduced motion makes selection immediate. No carousel or animation dependency.

Check desktop, compact mobile, all three locales, reduced motion, no JavaScript,
asset failure, intermediate scroll frames, keyboard navigation, anchors,
case-study links, downloads, and contact validation. Keep styling scoped to the
homepage. Preserve contact delivery, metadata, routes and existing hosting.
