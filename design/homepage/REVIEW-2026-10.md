## 2. The imagery, honestly

**Good**
- Seeker Radar, SOLitaire, Nuqta, Little Keepsakes: real captures, desktop and phone pairs, and the
  detail crops with captions. This is the standard everything else should meet.
- The constellation hero reads as premium at a glance and the mark-to-product reveal still lands.

**Weak**
- `go-to-nature-home.png`, `camp-and-hike-home.png`, `lulu-tokki-home.png`, `elsewhereco-home.png`:
  1× browser captures at 1440×900, the Go To Nature one still carries its cookie banner, and on a
  retina screen they are soft. They are shown `contain`-fitted in dark boxes, which is the "paper"
  feeling you noticed in the old hero.
- The constellation hero is a generated composite. The screens inside it are real, the devices and
  rocks are not. It is a poster of the work rather than the work, and it is static. Acceptable, but
  it will date, and it cannot be updated when a product changes without regenerating it.
- The four private builds (WhatsApp agent, Bab, Nedaa, Darbak) have no imagery at all and fall back
  to the three icon cards. The WhatsApp agent is the most on-brand project on the site and the least
  visual. Bab is the biggest thing you have built and shows as three icons.
- Case pages repeat the three architecture nodes twice (the top visual and the "System shape"
  section). On private builds that is the same content twice.

**What to do**
1. Recapture every web project with the capture pipeline from the hero experiment
   (`scratchpad/capture/*.mjs` pattern): 2× desktop, 2× phone, cookie banners dismissed, served as
   WebP. One evening of work. Replace the 1× PNGs.
2. Give every private build a real visual with seeded data:
   - WhatsApp agent: a rendered conversation replay (fake customer, real rules engine) as a short
     silent loop, plus the operator inbox with the takeover state visible.
   - Bab: the front door, the roles feed, the CV studio and a referrer packet, all with a demo seeker.
     Nothing from a real person.
   - Nedaa Lifestyle: Expo web renders of the member home, the schedule and the admin dashboard with
     test data; ask Nedaa before showing her brand photography.
   - Darbak: the web build once Tanzania is filled in.
3. Keep the constellation hero for now; when the products in it change, replace it with a layered
   version (background, devices, screens as separate layers) so the screens can be swapped and the
   layers can move at different rates on scroll, which the portal controller already supports.

## 4. Features worth building

Ranked by what they prove to a client, not by effort.

1. **"Talk to the agent" demo** (highest value). A simulated WhatsApp thread on the site, driven by
   the real deterministic rules layer with a demo catalog and no model key: a visitor types, sees the
   grounded reply, the price rule, and the moment it hands over to a human. Nothing leaves the
   browser. This turns the AI positioning from a claim into an experience. Two to three days.
2. **Agent replay loops**: 15–20 second silent captures per agent (WhatsApp sales, Telegram operator,
   Bab referral packet), using the video pipeline already in the repo. One day once the seeded data
   exists.
3. **Channel and stack filters** on the work index (WhatsApp / Telegram / Instagram / mobile / web).
   Half a day; the content model already has `stack`.
4. **"Now building" strip** generated from each project's `TASKS.md` phase line, exported as JSON at
   build time. Keeps the site honest without hand edits. One day.
5. **Project loops in the deck**: the deck shows a still per project; the hero experiment proved a
   loop fills a frame better than a screenshot. Reuse the `PortalVideo` component from commit a70f2bf (removed from the tree on 2026-10-16 with the rest of the hero experiment). Half a day per
   project after capture.
6. **Client words**: one approved sentence from Nedaa once the app ships, with her consent. Zero
   engineering.
7. **Instagram proof**: see section 1.

Not recommended: a live iframe of any product, a chatbot that talks to a real model from the site,
or page-wide scroll animations on ordinary copy. The brief's rule stands: one cinematic moment, then
a readable portfolio.

## 5. Motion and video rules

- Loops are silent, muted, `playsinline`, poster first, paused off screen, never under reduced
  motion. The `PortalVideo` component in commit a70f2bf already did this.
- One capture pipeline (Playwright, cookie banner dismissed, unpinned sections, 2× phone crop at
  390×844) so every project is recorded the same way.
- Animate media, not copy. Section reveals on text read as template defaults.
